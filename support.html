Frequently asked

Quick answers

The questions we hear most often. Don't see yours? Email us.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you record in AIPEC — holdings, transactions, receipts, goals, notes — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We do not operate a server, and we do not have access to your data. If you uninstall the app without exporting first, the data is removed with the app.

Can I sync AIPEC across multiple devices?

Not automatically. Because everything is kept locally, AIPEC does not sync between devices. If you want the same data on a second device, export a backup from the first device and restore it on the second.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings inside the app and choose Export. AIPEC creates a single backup file that includes your holdings, transactions, goals, and metadata. Receipts and attached images are also included in the export. Save the file somewhere safe — iCloud Drive, Files, or a personal cloud of your preference.

Does AIPEC connect to my brokerage?

No. AIPEC is a manual journal, not a broker integration. You record your own transactions. This is intentional: it keeps your credentials off third-party servers and gives you a clean record independent of any institution.

Are my receipts uploaded to a server?

No. Receipts you capture or attach live in the app's local storage and are protected by iOS data protection. They never leave your device unless you choose to export your full backup or share an individual image through the iOS share sheet.

Can I import transactions from another app?

AIPEC supports manual entry and CSV import for transaction history. Open Settings → Import to load a CSV file. We currently do not support direct integrations with brokerage APIs.

How do I delete all my AIPEC data?

The fastest way is to delete the app from your device. Because AIPEC stores everything locally, uninstalling removes all records, receipts, and goals. For a soft reset, use Settings → Clear All Data inside the app.

Is AIPEC investment advice?

No. AIPEC is an educational journaling tool. It does not provide recommendations, broker services, or personalised investment advice. Use it to organise your thinking; make your actual investment decisions with a licensed professional.

What iOS version does AIPEC require?

AIPEC requires iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. It is currently a portrait-only experience optimised for one-handed use.

How quickly will I get a reply?

We aim to respond within two business days. During launches and major updates we may take a little longer — every email is read and answered by a human, not a bot.
