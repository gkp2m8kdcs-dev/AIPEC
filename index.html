Built for clarity

Everything you need to think clearly.

A complete toolkit that respects your data, your time, and your intelligence.

📊

High-density dashboard

A hero net-worth card with period-over-period trends, a tappable donut chart for allocation, an interactive line chart you can drag across to reveal exact historical values, and a customisable bar chart of monthly net cash flow.

🔬

Deep analytics

Category-level returns, correlation heatmaps, K-line candlestick visualisation for watched symbols, and volatility scoring to keep risk in perspective — all without sending a byte off-device.

📸

Receipts that travel

Snap a photo of a trade confirmation or attach a saved screenshot directly from the transaction form. Receipts are stored locally, zoomable with pinch, and deletable with a long-press.

🧮

Six planning tools

Return calculator with CAGR and fee drag, compound interest projector with monthly contributions, retirement planner, multi-currency converter, correlation explorer, and DCA simulator that back-tests recurring purchases.

🎯

Custom goals

Track progress with circular rings and daily or monthly burndown charts. Whether it's a down payment, an emergency fund, or financial independence, AIPEC helps you see the path.

📤

Your data, exported

Export your full record anytime to keep your own backup. You own your data — AIPEC never locks you in and never sells you out.
